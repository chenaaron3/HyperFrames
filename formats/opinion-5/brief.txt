Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	The hook works because it reframes living alone away from loneliness and into a mental health win, which triggers a sense of validation and comfort for viewers who feel the same way but have not put it into words
	•	Open with a statement about what a lifestyle choice or change has done for your mental health
	•	List out three to four short and specific benefits, one per line
	•	Keep each line simple and personal, like quiet observations rather than big claims
	•	This format works across any niche. If you are not in the solo living space, you can use the same "what X has done for my mental health" structure and apply it to your own topic
2. Caption
	•	The numbered list format makes the content easy to follow and gives viewers a clear reason to save it for themselves or send it to someone who lives alone
	•	Repeat the same hook as a title at the top of the caption
	•	List out the same points again in list  to reinforce the message and make it easy to scan
	•	Add one or two relevant hashtags related to the lifestyle topic, such as living alone or solo living
3. B-roll
	•	Film calm and quiet moments around your space, such as sitting alone, making a drink, or relaxing on a couch
	•	Keep the pacing slow and peaceful to match the reflective tone of the text
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
