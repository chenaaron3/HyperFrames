Full script: I have no friends, I live alone, and I'm choosing to be single. The most common question I get asked is why. Surely there was something that made you this way, right? So hi, my name's Lana. This is part one of my life story that made me want to be alone forever. Have you ever heard the saying, never the chosen one? I used to think that saying followed me around in every aspect of my life. The first time I got a sense of that subconscious feeling was in my early childhood. I grew up on welfare and subsidized housing with a dad who abused my mom. When I say we had nothing, I truly mean we had nothing except the earwigs that were all over that house. My dad would leave us 10 days a month without any money. And my mom would try to find work, but not only could she not find work because of a gap on her resume, she also couldn't afford childcare. So my mom did what she thought was best for us and sacrificed her own happiness, allowing my father back into our lives just so we could afford food. I don't know why, but I was never a naive child and I always was aware of what was going on. Such a core memory of my childhood was seeing a full happy family for the first time. As a young child, you can truly only know what you've been shown. So when I saw that other kid outside of school with her parents, I remember thinking, wow, they truly must be the chosen one. Shortly after that day, my dad came back with so many promises like he always had. But something felt different this time. He took my mom, my sister, and I to Best Buy and he bought us an Xbox. Listen, for a family rationing $30 for two weeks at a time. An Xbox felt like a Lamborghini. My sister and I played on the Xbox that night. While my mom and dad sat on the couch happily walking. That night, I cuddled my toy Snoopy in bed. Fell asleep, happily feeling like the chosen one. But when I woke up in the morning, the Xbox was smashed and my dad was gone. All right. All right.
