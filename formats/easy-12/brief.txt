Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	List out a series of common life milestones or timelines, each followed by the harsh inner thought that comes with falling behind on it, written in quotation marks
	•	Close with a question such as "who wrote the rules" followed by a permission statement reminding viewers it is their life to define
	•	Keep each line short, the repetition of milestone and judgment is what builds the emotional weight
2. Caption
	•	Open with "I don't know who needs to hear this" to soften the tone and invite connection
	•	Write a few honest lines reassuring viewers they are not behind and there is no timeline to compare themselves to
	•	Close with an encouraging line about writing their own story, and credit the original creator if inspired by their format
3. B-roll
	•	Film simple daily life clips of yourself working, running, and being happy throughout your day
	•	Keep the footage warm and natural, showing an ordinary and fulfilling life rather than anything staged
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
