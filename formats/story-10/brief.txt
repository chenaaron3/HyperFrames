You can't love someone for something and then get upset at them for the same exact quality. Let me explain. My girlfriend is a perfectionist. And honestly, it's one of the things that I love most about her. She holds herself to a high standard and everything she does is thoughtful, intentional, and just always executed to perfection. You can't pick and choose when to love someone's core traits. If you celebrate them in one context, you have to learn to accept them in others as well. Otherwise, it's not really love.
