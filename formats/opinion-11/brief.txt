Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	"Today is someone's last day on Earth" is one of the most powerful pattern interruptions on Instagram. It stops the scroll immediately because it forces the viewer to confront something real
	•	Open with this one bold and unexpected statement creates urgency or emotion
2. Caption
	•	Very action focused - short but motivational and push the viewer to actually take action right now 
3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself doing things alone, whether that is traveling, eating, exploring, or just going about your day
	•	Each clip should match the energy of the line it appears with on screen
	•	Each B-roll clip shows the creator doing something alone while traveling, which visually reinforces the message and makes it feel lived-in rather than just words on a screen
	•	This format works across any niche. If you are not in the travel space, you can keep the same "Do it" structure and swap the opening line to match a fear or hesitation your own audience relates to
