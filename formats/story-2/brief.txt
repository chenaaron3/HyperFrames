Full Script: This is Makayla. Holy fucking... She is a 19-year-old unemployed dropout now. Why is she in such a pickle, you may ask? Well, just last month her creative writing professor told her to never try to become a professional author because she won't make it. And because Michaela is such a petty bitch that likes to clap back at men, she is determined to prove him wrong. So she dropped her classes for next semester, finally quit her mind-numbing job, and thought of an absolute banger book idea. Imagine a world where a witch strikes a deal with a crown prince that promises her freedom. Only she isn't after freedom. She's after revenge and has some crazy-ass plans to manipulate his feelings and infiltrate his palace through marriage to take down his family's corrupt legacy. Pretty epic, right? So Michaela has spent the past month writing to deliver this book to the die-hard fans of Powerless and Akotar. Because not only is she going to be a bestseller to prove her professor wrong, she is going to be a bestseller in the romanticy genre to haunt him even more. So she calls upon the book girlies that are not just interested in her story, but interested in forming an alliance. An army of book girlies, if you will, that think standing up for our art, stories, and dreams against mean-ass men is important. Michaela asks that you wish her luck and follow along if you're ready to see a journey full of imposter syndrome, writer's block, and most of all, really fucking hard work.
Notes:
Structure:
HOOK:  
This is [your name]
(Keep it as similar as possible to reduce variables. You can be creative with the clip, but something that shows your personality a little like hers is ideal!)

CONTEXT (With tension that introduces an authentic emotion, ‘spite’):
Share your struggle and create a curiosity gap. 
For example, Michelle used “living in a condo she definitely shouldn’t have signed for” (the immediate instinctive response is “why?”). Makayla used “she is a 19 year old dropout unemployed dropout now” (the instinctive response is “what happened? What’s going to happen now? What’s her story?”)

Don’t overthink this. The more complicated it gets, the harder it is to understand, especially with a fast-paced format like this. 
What pain point/struggle are you dealing with right now? Using something fresh is a great way to capture people's interest in your journey. 
If you don’t have any struggles currently, talk about the struggles you went through that got you here. 

Lastly for this part, an authentic emotion is also very much necessary. 

EXPLOSION:
A significant moment where an event/decision brings you from this struggle to where you are now. 
For Makayla, this was her being a “petty bitch” and deciding to drop her classes for next semester to prove him wrong and clap back at men. 

YOUR NICHE/JOURNEY:
This is where you dive in to talk more specifically to your niche. You’ve already gained the attention of people up until this point, so now you can call out to your niche more specifically. 
Talk about what it is that you’re doing right now (your journey), including specific things that relate to your page (eg, Makayla included her book since she’s a writer)

GOAL/MISSION:
Building on top of the last point, WHY are you doing what you’re doing?

CONNECTION:
This is the most important part to convert viewers to followers. You’ve earned their attention, now you have to earn their trust. 

Makayla forms a strong connection with the “book girlies” by saying that she’s forming an alliance to stand up for their art. This forms a very strong human connection with the viewer on the other side of the screen.

So form that connection by stating a shared goal. 

CALL TO ACTION:
To finish the video off, just add a classic call to action. Nothing fancy, just telling the viewer what they will get from following you and taking action. 

Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Super simple and introduce yourself immediately
	•	This signals the viewer that the video is personal and it’s going to be a story about your journey (and if they resonate with this journey, they will follow!) 
2. Caption
	•	Summarizes the whole point of the video 
	•	Add a CTA asking people to follow along 
3. B-roll
	•	The voice is “Father Christmas” from CapCut. 
	•	Don’t stress too much about the B-Roll, it does not have to be high quality at all. 
	•	If you cannot finish it in one day, don’t worry! It should take roughly 3 hours to complete this one, but you can definitely split it up to fit into the small pockets of time throughout your day. For instance, while you’re on a walk or driving, you can think of the script. When you have some time waiting, you can edit the video.
