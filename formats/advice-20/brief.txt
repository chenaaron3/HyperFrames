Caption:
Here are 10 everyday habits doctors wish people would stop doing 👇
1️⃣ Holding in your pee
Urine that sits in your bladder for too long creates a breeding ground for bacteria, which increases your risk of urinary tract infections and other bladder problems.
2️⃣ Sleeping with your mouth open
Mouth breathing can worsen sleep quality, dry your mouth, and contribute to snoring. Persistent snoring is also linked to higher blood pressure. 
3️⃣ Sitting with poor posture for too long
Hunching over for hours strains your back and neck, causing pain, stiffness, and potential long term issues.
4️⃣ Sitting on the toilet for too long
Sitting too long puts extra pressure on your veins in that area, which can lead to hemorrhoids. 
5️⃣ Constantly touching your face
Your hands pick up bacteria all day, and touching your face spreads it which can lead to skin irritation and acne.
6️⃣ Wearing tight headphones for hours
Tight headphones trap heat and sweat in your ears, which can irritate the skin and lead to infections.
7️⃣ Eating too fast
Eating too quickly can make you overeat and feel bloated because your brain hasn’t had time to realize you’re full.
8️⃣ Picking the skin around your fingers
This can create small openings in the skin that allow bacteria to enter, sometimes leading to infections like paronychia.
9️⃣ Using very hot showers too often
Hot water removes the protective oils on your skin, causing dryness, irritation, and sometimes itchiness.
🔟 Cleaning your ears with Q-tips
Your ears are self-cleaning, and wax naturally falls out. Q-tips just push it deeper into the ear canal and can irritate or even damage the ear drum. 
Which of these habits do you catch yourself doing? Be honest 🤔
