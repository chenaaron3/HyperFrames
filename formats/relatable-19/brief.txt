7 stages of moving abroad; 
Caption:
Stage 1: The “Emily in Paris” Phase
You just arrive, everything is beautiful, everyone looks like a movie star, and you’re taking 100 photos a day.
Stage 2: The “Every Weekend Trip” Phase
Flights are cheap, cities are close, and suddenly weekend getaways are your new hobby. Lisbon? Why not.
Stage 3: Wait… I actually live here?
After a month or two, novelty fades. Your metro rides, favorite park, and morning coffee start feeling… normal.
Stage 4: The Homesick/Identity Crisis Phase
You start missing home, questioning your choices, and wondering if this move was really right. (Spoiler; you will always be questioning yourself)
Stage 5: The Anxiety Phase
Life abroad isn’t all glamour; paperwork, appointments, and language barriers can hit hard. Your nervous system might be in overdrive for a while.
Stage 6: The Social Reset Phase
You meet tons of new people at first, but eventually you figure out who truly clicks and who drifts away.
Stage 7: The “I am actually thriving” Phase
Around six months in, you finally feel at home, find your people, and realize this new life is yours to shape.
If you are in any of these stages and feeling lonely don’t worry you are not alone. Welcome to our community, so tell me which phase you are in right now?
