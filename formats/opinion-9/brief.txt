Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with a bold and direct question such as "why not you?"
	•	It challenges the viewer directly and makes them question their own self doubt, then the list of affirmations builds momentum until the message feels less like a video and more like a personal push
	•	Follow with a list of short affirming statements that build momentum (directly from the speaker of the audio), each reminding the viewer of what they are capable of
	•	Ending with repeating the opening question one more time and answers with encouragement “believe in yourself”
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one line that reinforces the message of self belief
	•	No hashtags needed, the message carries itself
3. B-roll
	•	Film clips of yourself studying (or taking action in your own niche - (for example, fitness = film yourself workout)
	•	Let each b roll land on the beat drop to reinforce the momentum of the message
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
