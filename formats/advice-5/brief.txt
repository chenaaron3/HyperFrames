If I could go back, I’d start here.

If you’re between 25–35, start these 11 habits before it’s too late.

Simple. Grounded. Real.
The kind of habits that, over time, create peace, confidence, and freedom.

✨ Save this. Your future self will thank you.

1. When in doubt – move your body.
Go for a walk, dance in the bathroom, stretch, shake… move that energy. It will shift your state and bring clarity.

2. Morning self-care: non-negotiable.
Even 10 minutes. Prayer, stretching, sitting still with your thoughts, setting intention. It’s the difference between reacting to your day and creating it.

3. Master your mindset.
Thoughts become beliefs.
Beliefs become behaviors.
If you don’t learn to control your mind, it will control you.

4. Protect your energy (aka boundaries).
Took me forever to learn this. The more you say no to what doesn’t serve you, the more room you have for what does.

5. Discipline (I know, I know).
I used to think it was restrictive.
Turns out it’s the opposite.
Discipline is what gives you the freedom to actually do what you want.

6. Be intentional with your screen time.
First hour of the day and last hour before bed, keep them phone-free. Notice how much calmer you feel.

7. Doing things that scare you a little.
New cities. New hobbies. Saying yes when you’d usually say no. You find out who you are when you step outside what’s familiar.

8. Talking to yourself like someone you love. Would you talk to your best friend the way you talk to yourself? Yeah. That’s the work.

9. Get into gratitude practice.
It’s the best thing you can do for yourself.
I’m not talking about toxic positivity, I’m talking about noticing the small moments that actually shape your life.

10. Make a playlist of songs that support you through tough days. One right song can change the direction of your day.

11. Rest – on purpose.
When you alternate rest with productivity, your results improve.Rest is where ideas, clarity, and inspiration are born.

One last thing: Life isn’t happening to you.
It’s happening for you.
Even the hard stuff.
Especially the hard stuff.
Trust it.

With love,
Nina
