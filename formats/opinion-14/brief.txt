Caption:
I don’t really enjoy traveling as much as I did in my early 20s. And it’s taken me a while to realize that. 
I’m 28, and I’ve traveled now to 27 countries (on track to hit 30 countries by 30!). In my early 20s, I loved traveling and even interned at an airline where I got free flights all summer. 
But, as I’m getting older, I’m thinking more about how I prioritize my time, money and energy. And traveling doesn’t hit quite the same anymore. 
Here are the 3 main reasons why (would love to hear if you resonate):

1. After a certain number of countries, the novelty starts to wear off. Big cities feel the same, outdoor hikes feel the same, and new places aren’t as exciting anymore. 

2. I often travel without my fiancé or family, and even though the trip will be great, I really miss them. Is it really worth it to see so many places if they’re not there with me? I feel like I’m choosing between novelty/adventure vs rootedness/relationships. 

3. The cost of traveling is not just money anymore. It’s the cost of messing up my sleep and health routines, missing the comforts of home, and the toll on my physical and emotional energy. I can’t do multiple layovers, red-eyes or budget airlines / hotels anymore.

Overall, I feel like I’ve traveled enough. Especially during this MBA program, often times I’m going to a different country every month and it’s exhausting. I’m losing the joy that I had.
I say this, and I still have a few trips planned, but I’m realizing that travel is not a big priority for me anymore. And that’s ok! 
It’s so helpful for me now to have clarity about what does make me feel fulfilled and what I think is a good use of my time, money and energy. 
Would love to hear what you think!

Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	 An honest and slightly unpopular opinions are highly effective at driving comments and discussion
	•	It also goes against the usual highlight reel of travel content, and the promise of "3 reasons" makes viewers curious enough to read the full explanation
	•	Open with "X reasons why [activity] isn't fun anymore" using your own age or stage of life as context
	•	Add “read the captions” to directly tell the viewers to read the caption for the full explanation
	•	Keep it short and slightly vulnerable, the honesty is what makes people want to read on
2. Caption
	•	Open with an honest admission about how your feelings toward the topic have changed over time
	•	Share some context about your background or experience to establish credibility
	•	List out your reasons in numbered format, keeping each one personal and specific rather than generic
	•	Close with a reflective line about what you have realised instead, and invite viewers to share if they feel the same
3. B-roll
	•	It only needs a short reflective clip paired with a longer personal caption, all of the value comes from the honesty of the writing rather than complex filming
	•	This format works across any niche. If you are not in the travel space, you can use the same "X reasons why something isn't as fun anymore" honest reflection structure and apply it to your own topic
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
