Text Hook:
I’m 32…. and I wish I invested in these 5 assets in my 20s
(read caption)

Caption:
1. Real Estate (Paupahan)
Property that gives you monthly income.

Examples:
• Room Rentals
• Small apartment
• Airbnb unit

Why:
• monthly cash flow
• Tumataas ang value over time
• Kahit hindi ka active, kumikita

How to start:
Start small, pwede ka magpa-rent ng extra room or makipag-partner muna kung kulang sa capital.

⸻

2. Business / Side Hustle
Anything na kumikita na ikaw ang may-ari.

Examples:
• Online selling 
• Food business
• Service-based (design, printing, events)

Why:
• Unlimited income potential
• Ikaw may control
• Pwedeng i-scale

How to start:
Start with what you already know or skill mo. Kahit maliit, test mo muna bago mag-scale.

⸻

3. High-Income Skills
Ito yung asset na dala mo kahit saan ka pumunta.

Examples:
• Freelancing or VA
• Graphic design
• Video editing or content creation
• Social media marketing

Why:
• Low or no capital
• High income potential
• Pwede maging business

How to start:
Pick one skill, aralin online, then start offering your service kahit beginner ka.

⸻

4. Cash-Flow Assets (Low-risk, steady income)
Assets na nagbibigay ng consistent return.

Examples:
• Pag-IBIG MP2 Savings
• Government bonds
• Time deposits

Why:
• Mas stable
• Predictable income
• Good for beginners

How to start:
Mag-open ng MP2 account, then hulugan monthly kahit maliit and stay consistent.

⸻

5. Stocks
Owning a small part of big companies.

Examples:
• Jollibee Foods Corporation
• SM Investments Corporation
• BDO Unibank

Why:
• May dividends
• Long-term growth
• Pang build ng wealth

How to start:
Open a stock account (COL), then start small and invest consistently.
