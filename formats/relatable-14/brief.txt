Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	The hook works because it pairs a common negative thought with a calm and confident reframe right after it, which gives the viewer a quick mental reset and makes the content feel supportive rather than preachy
	•	List out a series of negative thoughts in quotation marks, each followed by a short and confident reframe on the next line
	•	Keep each pair short and punchy, the contrast between the two lines is what makes the format work
	•	Choose thoughts your audience actually has, the more specific and honest they are the better this lands
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one casual line that ties the whole message together
	•	No hashtags needed, the simplicity matches the tone of the video
3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself looking down or with a low expression for each negative thought, then cut to yourself smiling or laughing for each reframe
	•	Change the background or location between each pair to keep the video visually interesting
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
