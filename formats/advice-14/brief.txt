Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Use the template "what do I do as a [your role or hobby]? (in 1 min)" to set up a quick and specific explainer
	•	Talk fast and keep your energy fun and welcoming throughout, smiling even if the topic itself is not naturally exciting makes the video feel enjoyable to watch
	•	Cut a piece of fruit while you talk, this sets up a visual payoff that keeps viewers watching to see it finished, watermelon worked especially well but any fruit you enjoy works too
2. Caption
	•	Explain your job or a part of your life in a way that is fun but specific enough that people in similar roles or situations feel like "this is my person"
	•	Keep the tone casual and personal, mentioning small honest details about your process, like staying up late editing, helps you feel more relatable
	•	Add relevant hashtags related to your field or role
3. B-roll
	•	Keep production simple, you do not need heavy editing, focus on practicing the fast and fun delivery style rather than complex visuals
	•	Follow the b-roll as much as possible
