	•	"Fake Class" Mode Set your phone up, stand, and teach the wall for 5 minutes. Add weird diagrams. Explain like you're making a video. (You'll never forget what you taught.)
⸻
	•	Study Backwards Open your book from the last chapter. It feels rebellious, and it helps your brain connect dots in reverse. Great for concept-heavy subjects.
⸻
	•	The "Scribble Storm" Take a blank sheet, and write everything you know about a topic in 2 minutes without stopping. No notes. → Then look back and fill in gaps. Works better than passive reading.
⸻
	•	Study in 3 Rooms Change physical location every 45 minutes. Your memory will attach the topic to the place. Bonus: no burnout from sitting in one spot.
⸻
	•	Turn Doubts into WhatsApp Voice Notes Record your doubt/explanation and send it to yourself. Listen to it before bed. You'll absorb it like a podcast.
⸻
	•	"Catch Me If You Can" Timer Game Set a timer for less than you think you need (e.g. 15 mins for a 25-min task). Try to beat it. Speed + pressure = high focus. (Gamifies your brain.)
⸻
	•	Doodle Logic 🎨 For every topic, doodle something weird about it. Example: CPU = a chef in a kitchen. It doesn't have to be accurate. It just needs to stick.
