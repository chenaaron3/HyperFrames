Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with a common negative or self doubting thought your audience often has
	•	Follow with a list of short reassuring statements that counter the thought, each starting with the same word such as "you"
	•	Keep each line short and gentle, like quiet reminders rather than big declarations
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one warm and affirming line that ties back to the message of the text hook
	•	Add one relevant hashtag related to the emotional theme, such as self respect or self growth
3. B-roll
	•	Film calm and personal moments around your space, such as relaxing, reflecting, or going through small parts of your day
	•	Let the pacing stay slow and gentle to match the comforting tone of the text
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
