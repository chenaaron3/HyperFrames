Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with a numbered list title naming how many ways a specific mistake happens without people realizing it
	•	Triggers fear and curiosity that makes people wants to stay and watch and avoid the mistakes
	•	List out each point in a short and direct sentence (the pacing is perfect for the viewer to fully able to read each sentence - not too slow not too fast)
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one line asking viewers if they agree to invite discussion
	•	The creators also add relevant hashtags related to relationships and personal growth
3. B-roll
	•	Film simple cinematic style clips of a couple together, and some clips of just one person, matching the tone of each point as it appears on screen
	•	If you do not have professional camera equipment, simply film your clips horizontally to recreate the same cinematic feel
	•	The more important part is that the b-roll matches the text (the lists of mistakes), not the quality of the camera or the phone you’re using.
	•	This format works across any niche. If you are not in the relationship space, you can use the same "X ways people ruin Y without realizing it" numbered callout structure and apply it to your own topic
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
