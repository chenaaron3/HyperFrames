Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Structure your text hook in three phases using age stamps to show the passage of time
	•	Opens with a real and painful struggle voiced as a conversation with a parent, tracks the slow realisation behind that struggle across the years, and lands on a hopeful but honest truth about self worth, which makes the journey feel deeply personal and relatable
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short and heartfelt, one line letting viewers know the post is for whoever needs to see it

3. B-roll
	•	It only requires a series of age stamped text lines paired with photos from different life stages, no talking or complex editing needed
	•	Use old photos especially the sad ones to match the Normal phase at the beginning of the video
	•	Transition to photos with a lighter and happier energy for the Explosion phase
	•	Use photos where you are smiling throughout for the New Normal phase
3. Scripting 
	•	Follows the structure of normal, explosion, and new normal (click here for a more details of this storytelling structure)
	•	Normal: open with the early struggles and pain points your younger self experienced, framed as a conversation or confession to someone close to you
	•	For example, The original video was about her pain, her anxiety, and she described it as a butterfly in her stomach.
	•	17: “Mom, this is a bad feeling in my stomach that won't go away.”
	•	Explosion: describes the big realization or a turning point event regarding the struggle here, building up to the paid off 
	•	To continue, she talks about the events of her graduation, which makes her realize that anxiety will never stop.
	•	22: graduates
	•	Realises anxiety never stopped her from “performing”
	•	New Normal: showcase the paidoff with an honest learning and takeaway 
	•	The payoff is when she learned to be okay with having that anxiety and continuing to love herself unconditionally.
	•	24 “Mum, I think I'm getting better.”
	•	“The butterfly doesn't bother me as much anymore.”
