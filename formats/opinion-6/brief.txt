Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Use a three-line dialogue format, building up to a short and powerful final answer
	•	Keep each line short and let the last word or phrase carry the full emotional weight
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short and expressive
	•	One line that captures the feeling of the video is enough

3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself in as many different locations or settings as possible, cutting between each clip to match the beat of the music
	•	The more variety in locations, the better. Different spots around your city or daily life work just as well as different countries
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
