Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Avoid keeping the hook too general, a broad hook like "things to do alone at least once in your life" gives viewers no reason to come back to it even if saved, since it doesn't target anyone specifically
	•	Make the hook more specific by targeting a real pain point, and include a specific number to make it feel concrete and worth saving
2. Caption
	•	Keep each point short and sweet, adding a brief explanation of five words or less only if it makes sense, otherwise just list the point on its own
	•	Use a numbered list format so the points are easy to scan and follow
3. B-roll
