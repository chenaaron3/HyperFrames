Caption:
Exactly 21 years ago, my parents chose the unthinkable - to leave behind their comfortable middle-class life in Singapore and move our family to Brisbane, Australia. 
My brother and I were already studying in good schools, we were financially stable and had extended family around. They didn’t know anyone in Brisbane and would even have to run a business to be granted residency. So why? 
Looking back, my parents sensed that doing the status quo - pushing their kids to study hard, come first in class, get into the best university and be miserable their whole childhood - just didn’t seem quite right. Surely they thought there must be a different way to life where their kids could have better opportunities and the space to dream of their future beyond conforming to fit a mould in society.
So for years they adopted the immigrant life and started from nothing- sold vegemite sandwiches, learned how to cook fish and chips, worked the graveyard shift at the post office, all so we could have a better future.
20 years later, I realise my parents were teaching me the meaning of hard work, never settling for less. I’ve achieved things I wouldn’t have dreamed of if my parents hadn’t given up their comfortable lives. I practice in a suburb in Sydney where there are lots of Asian immigrants like me. As a bilingual English and Mandarin speaking orthodontist, I get to connect with lots of patients and their families with similar backgrounds and experiences, and help them understand their dental and orthodontic health in a way that comforts and reassures them.
I’m forever grateful to my parents for taking that risk.

Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Structure your text hook in three phases using age stamps (16:, 17:,) - key to signals to the viewer that there is a paid off coming
	•	This helps keep them stay and anticipating 
	•	starts with the raw and relatable struggles of a child navigating a new country (or your own specific niche), shares gratitude that reframes the entire journey, and then delivers a all achievements that make the viewer feel proud on the your behalf
2. Caption
	•	Tell the full story in the caption, starting with the sacrifice your family made and ending with what you have built because of it
	•	Keep the tone honest and grateful rather than boastful, the humility is what makes it resonate
3. B-roll
	•	Use old photos of your younger self to match the Normal phase at the beginning of the video
	•	It is okay if the old photos are blurry (that’s the point. This format works that way)
	•	Use recent b-rolls (photos or videos) that show your achievements and success for the New Normal phase
	•	This shows proofs and build strong trust 
 4. Scripting
	•	Follows the structure of normal, explosion, and new normal (click here for a more details of this storytelling structure)
	•	Normal: open with the early struggles and pain points your younger self experienced, these are the lines your audience will instantly relate to
	•	For example, for her the struggle was moving to Australia
	•	 “dad, why do we have to move to Australia?”
	•	“dad, they don't understand my accent.”
	•	Explosion: turning point, the moment of realisation “ah ha” moment 
	•	To continue, for her she realized how hard her dad works for her dream
	•	“Dad, you sacrificed so much…”
	•	“So I could have the chance to dream.”
	•	New Normal: the paidoff: showing your growth, achievements, and where you are today, ending with a closing line of gratitude
	•	She shows that she got into dream school and became the dentist
