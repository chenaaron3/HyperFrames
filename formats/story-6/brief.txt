Full Script:
I had everything a nice Chinese boy was supposed to be happy with. Top engineering degree, six-figure job, even a girlfriend who treated me right. But on the inside, it felt like my life was slipping away. One night, I found myself on my New York City fire escape thinking, is this what the rest of my life is going to feel like? And a week later, my relationship fell apart. While numbing my heartbreak with alcohol and video games, I decided I had enough. So I threw myself into the gym, losing 20 pounds and still feeling empty. I tried dating apps, got played, and deleted them for good. Then came side hustle after side hustle, which all failed. Until finally, I picked up a camera and just started recording my life. For the first time ever, a flame lit up inside of me. Maybe because for once, it wasn't on anyone else's checklist. And even before I had 100 followers, I wrote in my journal that I quit my job to chase this feeling. I spent all of my free time learning how to get better, and a month later, I quit my job to do content full-time. Just to grind day after day, seeing no results. And just when I thought I'd made the biggest mistake of my life, I earned $20,000 in my second month. For a moment, it felt like my life was finally coming together. But the truth is, the success just made me depressed. I genuinely considered ending my life. And as bad as that sounds, rock bottom was the best thing that ever happened to me. It forced me to sit with myself and actually ask what I wanted. I'm not fixed, nor am I perfect. But for the first time in my life, I'm building a life that truly feels like mine.




Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Opens a big curiosity in 2 ways
	•	The viewer will question “what does he have that he’s supposed to be happy about?”
	•	And “why is he not happy
	•	Mentioning “nice chinese boy”, he will attract the people who are “nice chinese boy” and keep them watching as well because automatically they will feel “this creator” gets me. 
2. Caption
	•	Genuinely sharing how surprised he is for how far he came 
	•	Gentle reminder to the viewer as well that so much COULD change in 1 year 
3. B-roll
	•	Plays a big part here every single b-rolls proves what he is saying is true 
4. Scripting 
	•	This video is really long because Richard keeps showing that, "Okay, I failed this. I failed this. I tried again." But for you, you don't have to make the video this long. We would suggest that you keep it with just three things:
	•	I fail at this
	•	I fail at this
	•	I started making videos, and I fail at this too. But it truly makes me happy.
	•	Follows the structure of normal, explosion, and new normal (click here for a more details of this storytelling structure)
	•	Normal: He started out the videos talking about having a life that any Chinese kid would be happy with: a job, a prestigious job, having a girlfriend. (He's established what is normal for him, what is normal for everyone who thinks about the Chinese kid.)
	•	Explosion: Then bam, he started talking about how inside he doesn't feel right and his relationship ended. He hated that life. (That's the explosion. This set the stage for the rest of the videos). He tried making videos and that actually makes him happy, but he couldn't make money from it and he failed and failed again. 
	•	New normal: But he refused to give up, and now for the first time, “I get to build a life that is truly mine” he ended the video. THE WIN.
