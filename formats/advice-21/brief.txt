Text hook:
If I had to move abroad again in my 20s, here’s how I'd do it:

Caption:
after 5 years abroad, here’s what I’d do differently:

– Do better research on programs, universities and the city to live in.
Studying in France felt so unreachable back then that I didn’t even think of aiming higher. I chose the safest options because I didn’t believe I could do more..

– Learn (or already have) skills that could help make money online before finding a job.
I was so sure I would quickly find a part-time job but then Covid hit:) I ended up with no income and a huge depression

– Practice the language as much as possible.
I was embarrassed to speak French, but without making mistakes, I would’ve never improved and improving was the key to building a life here

– Look for more opportunities everywhere: exchanges, scholarships, internships, volunteer programs… anything that could help with future career and make student years richer

– And the most important: have faith in my path
For years I doubted myself and compared my journey to everyone else’s. But now,5 years later, I can finally see that 

I was in the right place from the start, I just wasn’t able to see it yet

If you’re doubting whether you should move abroad: I’ll always tell you to take the risk. You can always buy a ticket home, but you might not always have the chance to try🤍

Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with "if I had to do X again in my 20s, here's how I'd do it" using your own experience as the topic
	•	Keep it short and reflective, the hindsight framing is what makes people want to read on
2. Caption
	•	Restate the hook as a short opening line, then list out what you would do differently using honest and specific points
	•	Include real struggles you faced alongside the lesson each one taught you, keeping the tone reflective rather than preachy
	•	Close with an encouraging line for anyone currently doubting their own path, reassuring them to take the risk
3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself standing alone at the airport, or in front of a beautiful view, keeping the mood reflective and calm
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
