Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with an incomplete statement such as "life is better when..." to create curiosity
	•	Reveal the answer on the second line, keeping it short and empowering
	•	Time the reveal so it lands exactly on the beat drop for maximum impact
2. Caption
	•	Keep it minimal, the text hook already carries the full message
	•	Add relevant hashtags related to fitness and motivation to reach a wider audience

3. B-roll
	•	Start with a two second selfie style clip of yourself running for the opening line
	•	After the beat drop, cut between different clips of yourself running and working out, changing the clip on each beat
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
	•	This format works across any niche. If you are not in the fitness space, you can use the same "life is better when" setup and reveal structure and apply it to your own topic
