Caption:
Save these important details for your next trip ✈️
1. The carry-on limit for liquid items is 100ml.
2. If you miss the first leg of your connecting flight, the rest of the flight is automatically canceled.
3. If there’s anything of value (e.g., diamond ring, camera, grad certificate), never check it in. Airlines lose luggage every day.
4. There are different charging ports in different parts of the world - you will need a universal charger.
5. The worst place to change your foreign currency is the airport.
6. Make sure your passport has enough pages and is valid for >6 months always.
7. The most annoying way to waste your time at the airport is by not having a pen. Keep one with you at all times.
8. Most countries will not let you enter their territory without having a return ticket. If you must buy a one-way ticket, get a second one-way ticket for your return.
9. You should take pictures of your passport, visas, and travel insurance and email them to yourself in case you lose them on a trip.
10. Speaking of travel insurance, you will definitely be needing it. Don’t forget to buy.
11. If you do not turn off your cellular data before you land in a new country, you may be hit with roaming charges.
12. Notify your bank of an upcoming trip to avoid your cards getting blocked abroad due to suspicious activities.
13. Pack light and aim to carry only a carry-on luggage. You save time and baggage fees.
I put these together after a couple of trips, and I’d like to think these are basic travel tips 101 everyone needs to know. Perhaps you needed a refresher.
Which of these do you already know? Or which will you be using on your next trip? Found this helpful? Share it! 🤍
