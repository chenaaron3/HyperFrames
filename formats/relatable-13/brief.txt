Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	the same line "it's just 45 min" is used for both sides, which forces the viewer to sit with the fact that the same amount of time can be spent two completely different ways
	•	Let the visuals do the arguing, the text should stay neutral rather than judgemental
	•	Keep the line short and casual, the repetition is what makes the message land
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one or two lines about how small choices add up over time
	•	No hashtags needed, the message carries itself
3. B-roll
	•	The format is easy to recreate because it only needs two clips filmed for each line, one showing the lazy choice and one showing the disciplined choice, no scripting or talking required
