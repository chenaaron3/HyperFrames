Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Label each item laid out on the floor with a short one word or two word text overlay
	•	Choose options that represent different ways you could spend your time, with one clear favourite that you will always end up choosing
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short and playful, one line that plays along with the idea that fate is making the decision
	•	No hashtags needed, the humor of the format carries the post
3. B-roll
	•	Lay out your items on the floor and film from above, spinning a bottle in the middle
	•	Every time the bottle points to something you do not want, kick it away and spin again, until it lands on the one thing you actually wanted all along
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
