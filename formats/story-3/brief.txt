Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Start the video off with a strong hook with a curiosity gap. Also, the stakes are high (a midterm + not showing up for week)
	•	This keeps the viewer wanting to find out “how is this possible?” or “what will happen to him”
	•	Think of a story that you want to tell and start with increase 3x the stakes, then walks the viewer to each step you take to solve the problem at hand  
2. Caption
	•	Funny and short 
	•	Matches with the vibe of the video and how the creator talks 
3. B-roll
	•	Focus HEAVILY on the B-Rolls that are filmed and the script. Do not mind the fancy lighting, editing, sound effects.
	•	For scripting, simply focus on normal, explosion, new normal. Don’t overcomplicate it with a 
	•	complex structure, just make it good not perfect. 
	•	For B-Rolls, as long as it supports/showing what you’re saying. Then you’re good
4. Scripting
	•	Follows the structure of normal, explosion, and new normal (click here for a more details of this storytelling structure)
	•	Normal = stressed before the midterm and haven’t been to class
	•	Explosion = figured out a method to make this work
	•	New normal = took the test, feel like he could breath again 
	•	Solves 1 specific problem 
	•	Documenting the whole process 
	•	Follows with a CTA (follows to see what I got) to open a new loop at the end
