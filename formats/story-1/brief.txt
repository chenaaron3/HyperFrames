1. Notes
	•	It’s a great format for showing off your transformation. This tells your story while at the same time establishing authority. This is a great format to practice with different messaging angles of your transformation until you find one that sticks!! (angles examples: struggled with smile and confidence, struggled with purpose in life, struggled with moving to a new country, etc)
	•	Don’t try to fit your story and transformation into her exact structure line by line if it doesn't make sense. I’ve outlined the general structure for you to follow, so you can fit your own message and story in there accordingly. Your story might differ from hers, your timeline and number of transformative events might differ, so use the structure as a guideline but you do not have to fit your story into the structure sentence by sentence!
2. Caption
	•	Introduce who you are. Your journey where it begins and where you are at now. 
3. B-roll
	•	A very easy way to time your B-Roll with her B-Roll is by making her video a base on the video editor timeline, then you can put your B-Rolls on top of hers whenever it changes. 
	•	Focus on your transformation as a person. Yes the main transformation that is most obvious is her smile, but she includes multiple dimensions of herself in here like starting her dream dental practice, growing closer to god, etc
