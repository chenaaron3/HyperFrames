Notes:
Execution
	•	Text Hook
	•	Script out one sentence that intrigues the viewer to keep listening, rather than stating something obvious or widely agreed upon
	•	Frame your topic in an unexpected way, for example instead of saying a skill or idea is simply useful, open with a personal moment that makes people ask "how" or "why" it changed things for you
	•	Caption
	•	Use bullet points rather than a full script, this gives you room to speak naturally about a thought that has been on your mind without needing to memorize exact lines
	•	Prioritize clarity over sounding smart, the goal is for the viewer to easily understand you rather than being impressed by complex wording
	•	Avoid stating anything too obvious or non controversial, the topic should make people curious rather than think "so what"
	•	B-roll
	•	Match your setting and delivery to the emotion of your message, if the tone is more reflective or melancholic then a quieter and dimmer setting works well, if it is meant to feel happy then bright and outdoor settings suit it better
	•	Follow the b-roll as much as possible
