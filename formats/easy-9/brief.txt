Notes:
Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with a line such as "life isn't easy when you have.."
	•	Follow with a short list of quiet personal traits in quotation marks, one per line, each one something your audience carries silently
	•	Keep each trait short and honest, the recognition is what makes it land
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one simple line that echoes the emotional tone of the video
	•	No hashtags needed, the simplicity matches the mood
3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself in a quiet and reflective moment, letting genuine emotion show as the text appears on screen
	•	Keep the pacing slow and still to match the heavy and heartfelt tone
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
