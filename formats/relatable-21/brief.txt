Execution
1. Text Hook
	•	Open with a common (yet uncomfortable) question people ask you 
	•	Keep this question (it is a format that proven to work)
	•	Reply with a bigger question that reframes the fear, flipping the concern back onto dependency instead
	•	The answer creates contrast, and the contrast is what makes the message land
2. Caption
	•	Keep it short, one reflective line about what you are actually afraid of, tying it back to living life fully rather than waiting for others
	•	No hashtags needed, the message carries itself
3. B-roll
	•	Film yourself doing everyday things alone, such as drinking coffee or walking, keeping the mood calm and confident
	•	Follow the original style as closely as possible
